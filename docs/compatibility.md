## What this means

**24 of 25 actions (96%) are `node20`.**

That collapses the compatibility problem. A runner that provides `/bin/sh`, `git`,
a Node 20 runtime, `tar`, `sha256sum` and network egress can execute the
overwhelming majority of marketplace actions — including the Docker ones, which
are Node wrappers that shell out to the `docker` CLI rather than requiring a
daemon of their own.

The `setup-*` actions do not need their toolchain baked in: they download it at
runtime. That is a deliberate design decision we can exploit — the base runner
stays small, and toolchains are fetched or overlayed on demand.
